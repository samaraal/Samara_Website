About Samara

Assisted living built around the person, not just the condition.

Samara is designed for residents who need support with daily life, recovery, mobility, medicines or companionship while retaining dignity, choice and family connection.

Our approach combines structured care routines with a warm, home-like environment. Every resident receives an individual care plan based on health needs, personal preferences and family expectations.

CompassionCare delivered with empathy
ComfortA safe and reassuring environment
DignityRespect for every resident
Who may benefit?
  • Elderly persons living alone or needing daily assistance
  • Residents recovering after hospital discharge or surgery
  • People requiring supervised medication administration
  • Persons needing physiotherapy or mobility assistance
  • Families seeking short-stay, respite or long-term care
