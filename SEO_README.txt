GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE
Prefer a Domain Property:
samaraassistedliving.com

Google will provide a TXT verification value. Add it in GoDaddy DNS:
Type: TXT
Name: @
Value: the exact Google verification value
TTL: 1 Hour

After verification:
1. Submit sitemap.xml in Search Console.
2. Inspect https://samaraassistedliving.com/
3. Test the live URL.
4. Request indexing.
5. Repeat URL Inspection for important pages such as services.html, contact.html, enquiry.html and careers.html.
