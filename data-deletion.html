Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 06 September 2026

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of Samara Assisted Living's website, Family Portal, WhatsApp communications or other digital services.

How to request deletion

Email care@samaraassistedliving.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request”.

Please include enough information for us to identify the relevant record, such as your name, registered mobile number and, where applicable, the resident name or Resident ID. For your protection, we may ask for reasonable verification before acting on a request.

What happens after a request

  • We will review and verify the request.
  • Information that is not required to be retained may be deleted, de-identified or otherwise made inaccessible.
  • Where deletion cannot be completed because a record must be retained for legal, clinical, financial, audit, contractual or regulatory reasons, we will retain only what is necessary and restrict its use accordingly.
  • Deleting an online account or portal access does not necessarily require deletion of official resident-care, billing or legally required records.

WhatsApp and third-party platforms

A Samara deletion request applies to information controlled by Samara. Copies or account information held independently by WhatsApp/Meta or another third-party platform are subject to that provider's own policies and controls.

Request help

If you need assistance with a deletion request, contact care@samaraassistedliving.com.
